Account creation uses server-side auto-confirmation and needs no SMTP. No live staff password or financial data was created or changed during verification.

After deployment, verify administrator-created staff login without email confirmation, administrator password replacement, non-admin/cross-shop rejection with real JWTs, deactivated staff sessions, Edge Function CORS, and simultaneous checkout/payment requests.
